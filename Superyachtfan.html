The DILBAR yacht is a 156-meter (512 ft) motor yacht built by Lürssen in Germany and delivered in 2016.

With a gross tonnage of approximately 15,917 GT, DILBAR is one of the largest private motor yachts ever built by interior volume.

Her exterior was designed by Espen Øino International, while the interior was created by Winch Design.
The yacht can accommodate up to 36 guests and carries a crew of approximately 96.

DILBAR has historically been closely associated with billionaire businessman Alisher Usmanov, but her present legal ownership and control require more precise explanation following a German court ruling in 2026.

Who Owns the DILBAR Yacht?

DILBAR is held through a corporate and trust structure connected with The Sister Trust, an irrevocable trust established by Alisher Usmanov in 2016 for estate-planning purposes.

According to information provided by Usmanov’s representatives, the trust is managed by an independent professional trustee, and neither Alisher Usmanov nor his sister Gulbakhor Ismailova currently owns or controls the assets held by the trust.

The ownership question became particularly important after European sanctions were imposed on Usmanov in 2022.

On June 11, 2026, the Administrative Court of Frankfurt am Main ruled in proceedings brought by the Lürssen shipyard against Germany’s Federal Office for Economic Affairs and Export Control, or BAFA.

The court ordered BAFA to determine that Lürssen was not required to treat DILBAR as a frozen economic resource, because there were insufficient grounds to regard the yacht as owned or controlled by persons included on the relevant EU sanctions list.

The ruling therefore means that DILBAR should not be described as a yacht owned or controlled by Alisher Usmanov for the purposes of those EU asset-freeze provisions.

Alisher Usmanov and DILBAR

DILBAR has nevertheless been publicly associated with Alisher Usmanov for many years.

Usmanov established The Sister Trust in 2016 and transferred valuable family assets into the trust structure, including DILBAR.

The yacht was widely described in international media as the Alisher Usmanov yacht or Usmanov yacht, particularly before the ownership structure became the subject of detailed legal and regulatory scrutiny.

Following the 2026 German court ruling, SuperYachtFan no longer describes Usmanov as the present owner or controller of DILBAR.

Read more about Alisher Usmanov, his business interests, net worth and historical connection with DILBAR.

DILBAR Yacht Design

DILBAR features exterior design by Espen Øino International.

She measures approximately 156 meters in length, with a beam of around 25.7 meters and a draught of approximately 6 meters.

Her steel hull and aluminium superstructure enclose approximately 15,917 GT of internal volume.

At the time of delivery, Lürssen described DILBAR as the world’s largest motor yacht by gross tonnage.

DILBAR Yacht Interior by Winch Design

The DILBAR yacht interior was designed by Winch Design.

The yacht can accommodate up to 36 guests in approximately 20 cabins, supported by a crew of around 96.

Winch Design created extensive guest accommodation and entertainment areas throughout the yacht, using bespoke materials and finishes developed specifically for the project.

Although selected interior photographs and design details have been published, significant parts of the accommodation remain private.

DILBAR Swimming Pool

One of DILBAR’s best-known features is her approximately 25-meter indoor swimming pool.

Lürssen states that the pool holds approximately 180 cubic meters of water and was the largest swimming pool ever installed aboard a yacht when DILBAR was delivered.

The yacht also includes extensive wellness, entertainment and recreational areas.

Helipads and Helicopter Operations

DILBAR has two helicopter landing areas, located forward and aft on the upper decks.

The yacht has historically operated with an Airbus H175 helicopter, providing long-range transport between the yacht, airports and suitable landing facilities.

DILBAR Engines and Performance

DILBAR uses a large diesel-electric propulsion system.

Her generating plant consists of four Wärtsilä 12V32 engines and two Wärtsilä 6L32D engines, producing a combined output of approximately 30,400 kW.

The system powers two propellers and gives DILBAR a reported maximum speed of approximately 22.5 knots.

The diesel-electric configuration also provides the substantial electrical power required for the yacht’s hotel systems, swimming pool, air conditioning, lighting and other technical equipment.

DILBAR and the 2022 Sanctions Dispute

DILBAR was undergoing refit work in Germany when sanctions against Alisher Usmanov were imposed in 2022.

Early media reports frequently stated that German authorities had seized the yacht.
German authorities subsequently clarified that the legal situation was more complex, and questions surrounding ownership, control and sanctions treatment continued for several years.

The yacht remained in Germany while authorities examined whether the vessel should be treated as an asset connected to sanctioned individuals.

German Court Ruling in 2026

On June 11, 2026, the Administrative Court of Frankfurt am Main issued an important ruling concerning DILBAR.

The case was brought by the Lürssen shipyard after BAFA had refused to confirm that the yacht did not need to be frozen under EU sanctions rules.

The court found BAFA’s refusal unlawful and ordered the agency to determine that Lürssen was not obliged to treat DILBAR as a frozen economic resource.

The decision was based on the conclusion that there were insufficient grounds to treat the yacht as an asset owned or controlled by a person included on the relevant EU sanctions list.

This ruling materially changed DILBAR’s sanctions status in Germany and is why SuperYachtFan no longer describes Alisher Usmanov as the yacht’s current owner.

Read the DILBAR ownership and court decision for more detail about the judgment and ownership structure.

Is DILBAR Still Seized?

Following the 2026 Frankfurt court decision, DILBAR should not be described as a yacht frozen in Germany solely because of ownership or control by Alisher Usmanov or another sanctioned person.

The yacht’s practical operating status may still depend on separate contractual, technical, regulatory or shipyard matters.

For that reason, SuperYachtFan distinguishes between the yacht’s sanctions status and whether she is presently ready or able to leave the shipyard.

Is DILBAR for Sale or Charter?

DILBAR is not currently known to be publicly listed for sale.

She is also not publicly offered for regular commercial yacht charter.
