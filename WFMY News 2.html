FORSYTH COUNTY, N.C. — Forsyth County's newly-elected District Attorney has released her first statement since Tuesday's midterm elections.

Democrat Amara Hunter, who won over incumbent Republican Jim O'Neill after receiving 54.43% of votes. O'Neill won 45. 66%. The change comes after O'Neill served as District Attorney for more than 15 years. He had worked for the Forsyth County District Attorney's Office since 1997.

In a statement to voters, Hunter writes, 

"Taking over the District Attorney’s Office is about more than a change in leadership. It’s about restoring confidence and creating a strong team that works together toward a common goal. My intention is not to come in and clean house. It’s to build on what works and address what needs to change to create an environment where people are empowered to do their jobs. There is important work ahead, and I look forward to getting started."

Hunter has worked as a police officer, assistant district attorney and public defender in the Forsyth County Public Defender's Office. During her campaign she said she hopes to serve everyone, especially victims of crime and families who want safer neighborhoods. 

"Together, we can build a justice system that protects our community, respects the law, and earns the public’s trust," writes Hunter on her website.

Current Forsyth County District Attorney Jim O'Neill said he wishes the next administration the best. He released a statement saying, 

"To every volunteer who knocked on doors, made calls, put out signs, and stood at the polls through early mornings and long days, I am forever grateful. You gave your time and your heart to this campaign, and I will always remember it. To every voter who supported us, thank you for your trust and your belief in the work we have done together.  It has been the honor of my life to serve as your District Attorney and to stand up for victims and families in this community. I wish the incoming administration success in the work ahead. The people of Forsyth County deserve the very best, and I am committed to a smooth transition."
